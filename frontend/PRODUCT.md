# PRODUCT: Map My Friends

Technical Luxury for Personal Connections.

## Users
- **Global Explorers**: Frequent travelers who navigate complex transit hubs (airports, stations) and value high-precision spatial tracking.
- **Private Networks**: Small, high-trust friend groups who demand sophisticated, encrypted, and visually elite location sharing.
- **Aesthetic Elitists**: Users who reject generic SaaS-flat interfaces in favor of instrumentation-grade depth and tactile responsiveness.

## Purpose
Map My Friends provides a high-fidelity spatial interface for tracking real-time movement, planned trips, and the global infrastructure (airports, transit) that connects private networks. It transforms location data from a utility into a premium digital experience.

## Strategic Principles
- **Physical-Digital Parity**: Interface elements carry mass, inertia, and momentum.
- **Instrumentation Over Software**: The app should feel like a high-end hardware tool, not just another app.
- **Privacy as Luxury**: Data protection is not a feature; it is the fundamental material of the experience (Proprietary LLC Standard).

## Tone
- **Precise**: Information is delivered with authoritative clarity.
- **Responsive**: The machine is "alive" under the user's touch (Thermal Glow).
- **Industrial**: Structural strength via typography and weighted materials.

## Anti-references
- **Cartoonish Maps**: No playful icons or rounded, bubbly aesthetics.
- **Flat UI**: Avoid the lack of depth common in standard Material Design.
- **Verbose Onboarding**: The interface should be intuitive to those who understand precision tools.
